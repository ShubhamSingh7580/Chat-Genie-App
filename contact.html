Write, Express Yourself and Get Things Done Effortlessly.

At BBAU Satellite Campus, Amethi, we believe that every student and faculty member should have a platform to express their thoughts, concerns, and ideas without any barriers. Our feedback form provides an easy and convenient way to share your views, whether it's about academics, infrastructure, facilities, or suggestions for improvement. By using this form, you can communicate directly with the concerned authorities and contribute to enhancing the overall university experience.

This feature is designed to give students and faculty a direct voice. Whether you want to share an achievement, report an issue, or express your views about university life, the feedback form is the perfect platform. Your ideas and feedback won't go unnoticed - they'll reach the concerned department, helping to create positive change faster.

Didn't Find Your Answer? Ask Here!

Our chatbot is designed to assist students with quick answers about university-related queries. However, if you didn't get the exact information you were looking for, don't worry! You can simply submit your question through the feedback form, and the university team will review and respond to it as soon as possible. This ensures that every student gets reliable and accurate information to help them navigate their academic journey with ease.

If the Chatbot didn't provide the exact solution to your query, you can ask it directly through the feedback form. This ensures that complex, course-specific, or administrative-related questions can be addressed by university staff. From exam-related queries to administrative clarifications, every question is reviewed carefully so that students can get the answers they need.

Report Issues and Suggestions.

If you have encountered any problems related to university facilities, academics, or administration, use this section to report them. Whether it's an issue with classroom infrastructure, Wi-Fi problems, maintenance concerns, or academic-related difficulties, your feedback will help the authorities identify and resolve them efficiently. Your voice can bring positive change and ensure that all students have access to the best possible resources and facilities.

If you've faced inconveniences in classrooms, libraries, hostels, or elsewhere on campus, use this feature to report the problem. For example, you can report if a lecture hall projector isn't working, if Wi-Fi is down, or if certain facilities aren't being maintained properly. These reports allow the university administration to address concerns quickly and improve campus life.
